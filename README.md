# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 28, 2026**

Divolandia Labs respects your privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains how our software applications handle user information.

## 1. Information We Collect

Our applications are designed to operate locally on your device and do not collect, store, sell, or share personal information unless explicitly stated otherwise for a specific application.

We do not require users to create an account to use our software.

## 2. Personal Information

We do not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, address, telephone number, passwords, or payment information through our desktop applications.

## 3. Internet and Network Access

Some applications may access your local network or the Internet when this functionality is required for the application's features.

Any network access is used only to provide the functionality described by the application.

## 4. Third-Party Services

Our applications may use third-party services when necessary to provide specific functionality. If an application uses such services, the applicable third-party privacy policies may also apply.

## 5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by our applications. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Our applications are not specifically directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

## 8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our applications, you can contact Divolandia Labs through our official GitHub profile.
